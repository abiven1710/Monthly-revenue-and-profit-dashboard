Sales Performance Dashboard
===========================

Dashboard Overview
------------------
This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of the company's sales, revenue, and profit performance using Microsoft Excel.  
It includes key metrics, monthly trends, and performance against targets to help management make informed decisions and track business goals effectively.

Tools and Techniques Used
-------------------------
Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Interactive Slicers and Filters
- KPI Cards
- Conditional Formatting
- Data Cleaning and Aggregation

Key Metrics
-----------
- Total Revenue: $2,568,720 (▲18.5%)  
- Total Profit: $514,680 (▲22.7%)  
- Profit Margin: 20.0% (▲0.7%)  
- Total Target: $2,600,000 (= +107K)  

Detailed Analysis and Insights
------------------------------

Monthly Revenue Analysis  
- Revenue shows a steady increase from January to December  
- December revenue peaked at $262,430  
- Monthly revenue consistently meets or exceeds targets

Monthly Profit Trend  
- Profit increased steadily throughout the year  
- Highest monthly profit recorded at $60,240  
- Trend indicates overall positive business growth

Sales vs Target  
- Sales consistently close to or above target  
- Peak performance observed in April and July with sales exceeding 120% of target  
- November reached 98% of target (+4% over previous month)

Revenue vs Profit  
- Monthly profit mirrors revenue trends  
- Highest profit recorded at $216.7K  
- Profit margins remain stable, indicating cost efficiency

Recommendations
---------------
- Maintain strong focus on high-performing months to maximize annual revenue  
- Identify low-performing months for targeted sales campaigns  
- Continue monitoring sales vs target to ensure goals are met  
- Consider strategies to further improve profit margins  

Conclusion
----------
The Excel-based Sales Performance Dashboard provides clear visibility into revenue, profit, and target achievement.  
It is a valuable tool for tracking business performance, identifying trends, and guiding data-driven decision-making.
